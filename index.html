Corporate Narrative

ELEGANT LANDMARKS, PROVEN TRUST

Apex Constructions & Infra Projects was established in Anantapur in 1997 with a vision to develop quality residential and institutional properties. Founded by Mr. B. Thirupalu, a civil engineering graduate (B.Tech, 1995), the firm has integrated rich engineering expertise with top-tier design sensibilities.

Under his visionary guidance, Apex delivered over 2 million square feet of premium spaces across Andhra Pradesh. Building upon this legacy, Apex Infra Projects was founded in 2019 to handle mega infra developments with high-tech in-house engineering support.

Engineering Guidelines

TECHNICAL BUILDING SPECIFICATIONS

Approved Standards

Every foundation and structural column is audited by certified external engineers to match national seismic structural codes.

Foundations as per structural soil report. Super Structure features premium R.C.C framed layouts and durable brick masonry. External walls (9" thick) and Internal walls (4½" thick) are structured using high-grade AAC Blocks with modern structural adhesive.

Main Door is framed with luxurious medium Teak wood and exquisite teak wood paneled shutters. All internal doors feature medium teak wood framing with high-resistance Laminated Flush shutters. WPVC water-resistant configurations for toilet frames. UPVC sliding windows are armed with strong M.S safety grills.

Premium vitrified tiles with 3" matching skirting in all main rooms. Corridors feature matte-finish non-skid vitrified tiles, staircase finished with solid rich granite, and private car parking built using heavy duty non-skid flooring. Painting features 2 coats of Premium Asian Tractor Emulsion over dual coats wall putty internally.

Standard concealed copper wiring and modern switches matching Finolex / Havells standards. AC power outputs in bedrooms. Plumbing utilizes high-durability Astral/Finolex pipes. Luxury bathroom taps and water closets are sourced from premium Jaquar/Cera or equivalent lines.
